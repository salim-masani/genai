Sick Leave Policy (Sample / Synthetic)

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Unused sick leave does not carry over into the next year.

How to apply for sick leave:

1. Notify your manager as early as possible, ideally before your shift starts,
   and give an estimate of how long you expect to be away.
2. Record the absence in the HR portal under "Sick Leave".
3. For an absence of more than three consecutive days, upload a medical
   certificate from a registered practitioner.
4. Keep your manager updated if your expected return date changes.

Sick leave may be used for personal illness or to attend a medical appointment
that cannot be scheduled outside working hours.

This document is synthetic sample content created for classroom demonstrations.
It is not a real company policy and is not medical advice.
