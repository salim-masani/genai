Annual Leave Policy (Sample / Synthetic)

Full-time employees are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year. Leave accrues at 1.75 days per completed month of service.

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar
year. Carried-forward days must be used within the first quarter or they are
forfeited.

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rated basis according to
their contracted hours.

To request annual leave, submit a request through the HR portal at least two
weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines the request based on team
coverage.

This document is synthetic sample content created for classroom demonstrations.
It is not a real company policy.
