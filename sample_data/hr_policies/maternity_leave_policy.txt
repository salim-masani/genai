Maternity Leave Policy (Sample / Synthetic)

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave for the
birth or adoption of a child.

To be eligible, an employee must have completed at least 12 months of service
and worked a minimum of 1,250 hours during that period.

Leave may be taken as a single continuous block or intermittently, subject to
manager and HR agreement. Health insurance benefits continue at the same level
during maternity leave, and employees continue to accrue annual and sick leave.

Employees should notify HR at least 30 days before the planned start of leave
when the need is foreseeable.

This document is synthetic sample content created for classroom demonstrations.
It is not a real company policy and is not legal advice.
